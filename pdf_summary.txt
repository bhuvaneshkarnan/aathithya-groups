OUR COMPANY 
Sri Aathithya Infra Project Private  Limited is a dynamic and professionally 
managed infrastructure company delivering high-quality Design Engineering, 
Construction, and Contracting solutions across Tamil Nadu. 
 
With a strong presence in the southern region,  the company has  successfully 
executed a wide range of projects including mass earthworks, road construction, 
building design & execution, site development, transportation  works, road 
furniture installation, and rock cutting & filling operations. 
 
 
Driven by a commitment to quality, timely delivery, and cost efficiency, Sri 
Aathithya Infra has earned a reputation for reliability and technical excellence. The 
company leverages modern construction practices, experienced manpower, and 
robust project management systems to deliver value -driven infrastructure 
solutions. 
Our focus is  on building long -term relationships with clients  by consistently 
exceeding expectations and contributing  to the development  of sustainable 
infrastructure.

INTERNATIONAL MINING PROPOSAL 
Mining Division – International Expansion (Zambia Project) 
Our Mining Department has proposed the establishment of Sri Aathithya Blue Metals 
as part of a new international venture, in collaboration with local mining partners in 
Zambia, with operations centered in Lusaka, the capital city. 
The project involves the development of a large-scale gold mining operation across an 
area of approximately 800 acres under a mining lease. 
We are pleased to report the following progress: 
 
Stage I Clearance has been successfully obtained from the Government of Zambia. 
The prospecting mining lease has been completed. 
 
We are currently awaiting Stage II clearance, which will enable us to commence full-
scale mining operations. 
The final lease sanction is under process with the concerned Zambian ministerial 
authorities. 
 
ProjectTimeline: Subject to receipt of final approvals, the gold mining operations are 
expected to commence by the end of 2026
