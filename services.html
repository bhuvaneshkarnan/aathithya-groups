Our Core Infrastructure Disciplines

Eight integrated infrastructure capabilities delivered completely in-house with wholly owned plant, heavy machinery fleet, and captive quarry resources.

01
Highways & Expressways

Highways Construction (NHAI & MoRTH)

Turnkey engineering and execution for national highway corridors, bypasses, and capacity expansions meeting rigorous MoRTH standards.

Recording initial levels with client survey teams
Mass earthworks & embankment cut-and-fill
Soil transportation, grading, curing & compaction
GSB, WMM, DBM & BC laying with sensor paver machines
Construction of small box culverts & minor bridges
Lane markings, road furniture & canopy signboards
Enquire About Highway Works
02
Railway Infrastructure

Railways Works

Specialised civil contracts supporting railway corridor expansion, earthwork formation, track subgrade stabilisation, and railway level-crossing approaches.

Railway embankment bed cutting, filling & compaction
Track sub-ballast supply & mechanical spreading
Road-over-bridge (ROB) & under-bridge (RUB) approach civil works
Retaining walls, side drains & slope protection structures
Enquire About Railways Works
03
State & Rural Networks

State Highway Construction & DRDA Works

End-to-end execution of State Highways Department, District Rural Development Agency (DRDA), and PMGSY road networks.

State highway widening, strengthening & black-topping
Rural roads under Namakku Naame Thittam (NNT) & PMGSY
Shoulder reprofiling, grading & automated roller compaction
Maintenance of road assets & right-of-way (ROW) cleaning
Enquire About State Highway Works
04
Quarry, Blue Metals & Gold Mining

Mining of Major & Minor Minerals

Pioneers in aggregate extraction, operating a 75-acre captive stone quarry, 350 TPH 3-stage crusher plant, and 300 TPH sand wash plant.

International Mining Division (Zambia Gold Project): Proposed 800-acre large-scale gold mining operation in Lusaka, Zambia under mining lease. Stage I clearance secured from Government of Zambia; operations planned by end of 2026.
Overburden & waste rock removal
Controlled rock blasting with utmost safety
350 TPH crusher plant aggregate sizing
300 TPH sand washing for certified M-Sand & P-Sand
Order Aggregates / Mining Enquiries
05
Retail Outlets & Petroleum Clearance

Approach Road for Fuel Pump & PESO License

Complete turnkey development of statutory highway access, ingress/egress deceleration and acceleration lanes for petrol pumps and commercial establishments.

IRC:12 normative geometric layout & profile engineering
Approach road asphalt paving, culvert crossing & curbs
Coordination for PESO (Petroleum & Explosives Safety Org) approval
NHAI & State Highways NOC clearance documentation
Consult for Fuel Pump Road & PESO
06
High-Voltage Power Corridors

EHT / HT Line Shifting & Utility Shifting Works

Critical-path utility diversion along highway rights-of-way (ROW), executing extra-high tension (EHT) transmission tower foundations, fabrication, and stringing.

Land acquisition & liaison with TANTRANSCO / Power Grid
Stud fixing, pile foundations & rock anchoring
Tower erection & conductor tension stringing
Obtaining Line Clearance (LC), load transfer & testing
Municipal water & utility pipeline relocation
Enquire About EHT & Utilities
07
Heavy Logistics Fleet

Transportation & Bulk Carriers Services

Wholly owned heavy transport fleet powering timely, large-volume haulage of excavated earth, rock boulders, aggregates, and ready-mix concrete.

Fleet of 32 heavy tippers & 15 rock dumpers
5 high-capacity water tankers for dust suppression & curing
3 transit mixers for mobile concrete placement
Dedicated service vans and mobile mechanic units for 100% uptime
