Founder's Journey & Vision

Pioneering Disciplined Infrastructure Delivery Across South India

In 2018, S. Kannan initiated our contracting journey through M/s Kannan Contractor. Operating initially with lean manpower and sheer operational discipline, he demonstrated exceptional on-ground execution capabilities and transformed contracting challenges into monumental infrastructure opportunities across Tamil Nadu.

Anticipating the strategic need for total supply-chain self-sufficiency, he pioneered the establishment of Sri Aathithya Blue Metals — commissioning a 350 TPH 3-stage cone crusher plant and an exclusive 300 TPH sand wash facility on a 75-acre captive quarry — followed by Sri Aathithya Sands to dominate regional M-Sand and P-Sand manufacturing.

"Nation-building demands an uncompromising devotion to quality, time-discipline, and wholly owned mechanized capability. We do not just lay roads and erect transmission lines; we build enduring foundations of trust for communities and authorities alike."
— S. Kannan, Founder & Managing Director

Under his strategic stewardship, Sri Aathithya Infra Project Pvt Ltd has scaled audited turnover from ₹47 Lakhs (FY 2020–21) to over ₹41.29 Crores (FY 2025–26) across 11 on-going NHAI, MoRTH, and PMGSY contracts, alongside an international mining expansion in Lusaka, Zambia.

Our Sister Concerns

Four specialized companies operating under unified leadership.

M/s Sri Aathithya Blue Metals

District pioneers in aggregate production, operating a cutting-edge 350 TPH crusher plant and an exclusive 300 TPH sand wash plant supplying high-grade materials across the region.

M/s Kannan Contractor

Initiated construction operations in the year 2018. Built on disciplined execution and lean manpower, creating major infrastructure contracting opportunities across South India.
